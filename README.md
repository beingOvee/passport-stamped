# Passport Stamped

**By [The Duo Explorer](https://www.instagram.com/theduoexplorer)**: travel stories, honest guides, and unforgettable moments.

Passport Stamped turns the places you've visited into a personal travel map.

- **My World:** mark the countries you've visited, out of 195 (the 193 UN member states plus Vatican City and Palestine). Territories such as Greenland, Hong Kong and Puerto Rico can be marked as other places.
- **My UK:** go deeper across 361 local authority areas in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
- **Memories and next stops:** add visit dates and notes, and keep a list of where to go next.
- **Share cards:** download your passport as an image for your feed or story, in square, portrait or story sizes.

No account is needed. Everything is saved in your own browser. You can export a backup file to keep a copy or move your passport to another device.

## Map data

- **Countries:** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), 1:50m Admin 0 countries. Public domain.
- **UK areas:** Office for National Statistics, Local Authority Districts (December 2024), licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
